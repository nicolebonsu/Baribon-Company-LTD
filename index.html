The Legacy of Exclusivity.

Baribon Company Ltd is a premier luxury interior design firm specializing in the creation of sophisticated, elegant, and timeless spaces. We are dedicated to transforming interiors into refined environments that embody comfort, elite status, and exceptional craftsmanship.


With an uncompromising eye for architectural proportion and a profound passion for design excellence, Baribon delivers bespoke, turnkey environments tailored specifically to mirror the high-stakes lifestyles and global aspirations of business executives in the heart of Accra.

Nana Serwaa Bonsu

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

"True luxury is an experience. It is the understated harmony of texture, balance, and fine artistry coming together to form a personal sanctuary."

As the sole driving creative force and Principal Designer behind Baribon Company Ltd, Nana Serwaa Bonsu has carved out a distinct niche within West Africa’s elite luxury residential sector.

Her signature design philosophy masterfully bridges uncompromising, clean contemporary minimalism with the quiet grandeur of organic African materials. By maintaining a highly selective, boutique operational model, Nana Serwaa guarantees every client receives her undivided focus—bringing a flawless, hands-on masterclass execution to major executive construction campaigns across Accra.
